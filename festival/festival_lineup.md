# Imaginary Festival — 3 Days, 2 Stages

Curated 2026-08-29 from live-shows tracking data (follows, show history, fast track, research queue).
Format: 45-minute sets, 15-minute changeovers. Second stage on the hour, Main stage on the half-hour
(so the Main closers - Kingfish, Batiste, Larkin Poe - end each night last).
Final two acts on each stage each night are favorites-list artists. No act plays twice. 56 acts.
