# Azgaar leg — using heightmap.png

Generated by `emit_heightmap.py` from `fantasy_map_data.json` (regions + waterways),
2000x1400, grayscale, seed 2026. Lighter = higher; everything below ~18% luminance
is intended as sea. Regenerating after any data change, from anywhere:
`python3 tools/map/build_fantasy_map.py && python3 tools/map/emit_heightmap.py`

## Import flow (azgaar.github.io/Fantasy-Map-Generator)

1. Generate any map, then open the heightmap editor (Tools -> Heightmap -> Customize / Edit).
2. Choose the **Image converter** tool, load `heightmap.png`, and apply. Tune the
   conversion so the sea threshold lands around the 18-20% luminance mark - the
   southern sea, the western bight, and the six-island archipelago should all read
   as water, and nothing inland should flood except the two river mouths.
3. Exit customization and let it regenerate rivers, biomes, and relief. The carved
   valleys should pull its two main rivers roughly along the Big Muddy and
   Slowhand Creek lines; if a river ignores a valley, deepen that valley in the
   emitter (the 0.12 carve factor) and re-import.
4. In Options, drop states/burgs/labels to zero or near it - settlements come from
   your overlay layer, not from FMG's generated towns. Religion/military layers off.
5. Style to taste, then export SVG or PNG at 10:7. Exported at 2000x1400 (or any
   10:7), the overlay JSON's 1000x700 coordinates map on with a single scale factor.

## Geography checklist against the data model

Landmask-first: a keep-land field from every settlement position (plus river corridor
and range core) guarantees inhabited ground stays dry; noise and directional sea biases
own the coastline. Check for: the massif's north end as a promontory - the northeast sea
closing in from north and east, a channel biting behind the cape, the Outer Isles (one
island per isles district plus skerries) trailing the range's line offshore; the central
north coast making inroads around a short coastal range (the Chain) whose north face is
the shore of the Chainwater Sound; a fjord coast on the west with fingers cut on the
SW->NE diagonal toward the forest lake; the northwest corner as open sea holding one
largish undiscovered island; the east shore slanting southwest toward the bay with
irregular bites; the ragged southwest with its barrier lagoon; and the single river
system - forest lake, Slowhand Creek, confluence, Big Muddy past the Steel Foothills -
emptying into the round sheltered harbor between two spits, small harbor island inside.
If FMG's conversion shifts any of this, fix the emitter rather than hand-editing.

## Hand-finishing

FMG's export can go straight under the JS/CSS overlay, or take a detour through
Inkarnate for stamp-and-texture artistry - trace FMG's coastline as the land shape,
keep the terrain placement, and export from there instead. Either way the strict
rule from the design prompt holds: no towns, roads, or labels in the background;
those belong to the data layer.
