# taste_profile.md — private research context

Free-form context an agent reads before doing artist research for you.
Suggested sections — rewrite entirely in your own voice:

## Genres and lanes
(What you buy without hesitation; what's adjacent; what's out of scope.)

## Venues and geography
(Preferred rooms and scale; distance limits; standing vs seated rules.)

## Tier model
(What Strong / Medium / Lower mean for purchase intent in YOUR system.)

## Budget posture
(Monthly cap, multi-ticket rules, what counts as shared.)

## Discovery sources
(Friends, DJs, festivals, similar-artist graphs you trust.)
